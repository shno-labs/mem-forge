# Complete claim and Evidence extraction validation

Status: experiment in progress; no runtime/deployment acceptance.
Date: 2026-10-02. Canonical OSS baseline: `57f8b006962bf65a6430592b1a5002e15082c8c1`.
Design: [proposed ADR 0046](../adr/0046-separate-evidence-correspondence-from-citation-presentation.md).

## Contract and experiment boundary

The goal is complete useful claim coverage together with faithful qualifications,
atomic assertions, direct Primary, necessary Required and readable pinned
citations. Candidate admission does not repair selections, trim refs or reject
valid knowledge for citation clutter. Model response counts and mechanical group
accounting do not certify semantic coverage.

Real retained Confluence/Jira content and pinned repository documents are read
in full. Provider text, personal identifiers, credentials and raw outputs remain
in the local private experiment packet. Calls use the verified CF dev app's
`memforge-aicore` binding, online `sap/anthropic--claude-4.6-sonnet`, the deployed
Cloud structured-client factory and canonical OSS schema transport. No production
Memory, source revision or lifecycle record is written.

This experiment uses the production projection-context, complete ReadingGroup
planner, MemoryExtractor and result assembler, with an isolated normalized
source-neutral adapter. Proposed compound mappings and origin schemas are
injected private prototypes. This proves neither native provider adapter history
nor persisted compound-selection/resource parity. The existing current-input
cache is not immutable historical authority.

## Source-only protocol

Two independent agents read complete sources and froze inventories before new
generation: A has 152 obligations, B has 100. Their different atomic granularity
is preserved and scored separately; no candidate count is the expected Memory
count. A source-only adjudication retains the clear existing UnitTitle policy
as a testable source-content assertion, independently of the proposed stricter
runtime citation policy. A stale one-call statement stays in original scoring
and as a source-conflict diagnostic; current-runtime scoring requires complete
ReadingGroup execution and intact indivisible-group capacity. No generated
output was read to change these decisions.

The accepted ADR 0043/0034 Value amendment supersedes the extraction prompt's
remaining older six-month/refactor and code-recoverability exclusions. This
contract drift was identified and corrected before generation and inventory
freeze. It is not a relaxed bar chosen after seeing model output.

Blind reviewers receive original full sources, anonymously identified original
candidates, untruncated selected citations and exact dependency spans. They do
not receive treatment identities, prompts, implementation, hypotheses or peer
judgments. Semantic entailment, qualification, selected support, atomicity,
Primary contribution, Required necessity, Value, duplicates and readability are
separate axes. Clutter alone does not erase otherwise grounded knowledge
coverage. Complementary valid claims may jointly cover an obligation.

## Origin composition experiment

A revised prototype reads every complete ReadingGroup into provisional source
assertions, then rereads the complete Unit to compose atomic qualified Memories
and selections. Every provisional assertion gets an explicit outcome; splitting
one draft into several Memories is allowed. Outcomes are origin reading data,
not admission rejection or new persisted state. Independent evaluation checks
that an unsupported-draft disposition did not discard valid lasting knowledge.

Immutable source scope is mapped into citations with exact source receipts.
Private retained exported-title fixtures have explicit declaration contracts;
this is not generic first-heading inference or verified native-provider title
retrieval. Display-only UnitTitle changes leave bindings unchanged. A controlled
actual source-title change preserves 48 local row material matches but changes
all 48 interpretation bindings, preventing blind Support reuse.

Three documents completed six online model calls and emitted 99 original
candidates. A independently reviewed 99 candidates, 69 frozen obligations,
101 provisional outcomes and citation views, with complete coverage **63/69**
(Confluence 42/44, Jira 1/1, ADR 0045 20/24). B reviewed the same full packet
against its 54 frozen obligations: **46 complete, 5 partial, 3 missing**;
atomic complete coverage was 28/54. A identified 29 bundled candidates and one
unnecessary Required. Both validated the pinned source identity/scope values,
which closed previous ambient-title gaps, and both still failed acceptance.

Named residual violations include detached comments missing scenario identity,
unselected overarching rules, independent rules combined with transient status,
configuration field meanings changed, links promoted to external authority,
confirmation/discovery phase errors and retention bounds promoted to absence.
The only non-represented draft was incorrectly classified `not_source_asserted`,
but its underlying scenario rules remained represented; neither reviewer found
unique lasting knowledge lost through that outcome. This is not permission to
accept the incorrect classification. Original scores and disagreements remain
preserved; the denominator is not reduced to pass. The prototype explicitly rejects
multiple planned requests and checks whole-current scope, no skipped groups and
no missing authorized anchors before declaring its catalog complete. It cannot
certify the multi-request Unit-wide closure contract. The public design requires
ordered whole-Unit closure rather than rereading each local request twice.

## Facet protocol falsification and source ownership correction

The next complete three-source attempt made nine successful online model calls
and retained 107 intermediate facet claims (57 Confluence, 11 Jira, 39 ADR 0045).
Its whole-Unit operation failed the literal-copy scratch-witness check. No
completed extraction or production Memory was published. A saved-output,
zero-model replay reproduced `witness_not_verbatim_in_selected_mapping`; it is
a diagnosis, not a successful new-schema online repetition. Failure-result
serialization initially masked that typed source-formation failure with a
`ValueError`; original records and diagnosis remain preserved.

The correct ownership is application-owned exact source text/spans plus
model-selected refs, not a second LLM copy of that same text. Removing an
unnecessary scratch-copy prerequisite does not remove any semantic gate:
selected source must still entail every facet/name/time property, Primary must
be direct, Required necessary and source coverage complete. The replacement
schema is a new frozen hypothesis requiring new online calls, never a repaired
score for the old protocol. Its origin contract must enter derivation/work
identity; an old outer prompt digest cannot safely cache changed inner schemas.

The grammar-scoped compiler also contained a wrong assumption: ordinary HTML
`pre/code` are formatting containers, not native raw text. Five authored Jira
macros remained raw XML in those containers. Their material was retained, but
readability failed. Official HTML, Atlassian and CommonMark semantics require a
representation-owned content mode: Markdown native code is literal; HTML
formatting has decoded children; provider plain-text/CDATA is literal under its
explicit native descriptor. A separate zero-model probe restored all 41 stored
status titles and 34 issue keys in the 11 real tables with seven content-mode
controls. It does not yet prove a complete corrected runtime bundle. Old frozen
code/control results are not rewritten to hide this false assumption.

## Adapter ownership and resource/execution boundaries

Native grammar and field meanings belong to the source adapter or explicitly
named provider-format module. Standard Markdown/HTML structure, source integrity,
matching and Memory semantics remain shared. A centralized registration table is
not itself a defect. A Confluence-hosted Jira macro is Confluence grammar, whereas
Jira REST/wiki fields are Jira-owned. Current shared boilerplate text deletion,
Jira field schemas and `fromString/toString` instructions in the generic extraction
prompt do violate this target boundary; a multi-provider projection method is an
adapter organization issue, not proof of extraction-layer leakage.

Current resolved selections, serialization, SQLite/HANA references and HTTP/tool
DTOs cannot carry compound scope/header/value mappings. Read-only running CF HANA
source confirms parity with the current OSS excerpt/hash fields, rather than the
older local Cloud checkout: this is a shared protocol gap, not proven deployed
HANA drift. Existing content links read the current Unit input; they are not
pinned complete citation resources. A separate private protocol prototype passes
16 SQLite close/reopen, full-binding validation, current visibility, source/revision
membership, historical-resource, tamper and missing-resource cases. It invokes the
frozen provider-owned verifier for real mapped source. This is design feasibility,
not live HANA/HTTP or production compound-storage acceptance.

A Unit-owned private executor uses the production runner and unchanged actual
route budget (1,000,000 input/context, 64,000 output metadata, fraction 0.8,
32,768 requested stage output). Synthetic transport inputs induce four survey,
four joint-reading and four formation requests; exact journal replay adds no
calls and failure returns no aggregate candidate subset. This does not establish
real multi-request online semantics. The production per-request extraction owner
and skip-and-commit behavior require an upstream Unit contract change.

An actual pinned repository PNG has verified original bytes, whole-Artifact
projection and production image loading/transport. Its source-only inventory
contains 82 assertions about the diagram's authored target design, including
historical policies; examples/legend colors are excluded before generation.
Visual semantic validation remains open. The existing reserve is only 1,280 tokens
because it measures short Artifact presentation rather than visual content; the
private whole-source visual task declares 32,768 demand within the unchanged cap.
This task-demand correction is not a provider-limit or source-type workaround.
Image source revision/hash and transport policy must also enter request/work
identity; prompt/schema identity alone cannot distinguish missing image inputs.

The whole-source Artifact origin experiment completed three actual online
Sonnet operations with unchanged original source/transport image binding: survey,
joint Unit read and formation. It returned 37 candidates, with zero retry or
fallback and 20,319 input plus 11,872 output tokens in successful telemetry.
The complete original output, roles and 28 provisional outcomes were frozen for
two independent reviews against all 82 pre-generation source obligations. Both
reviews fail: each finds full content coverage of 46/82 and 13 contradicted or
materially distorted obligations. One reports 16 partial and 7 missing; the

semantic acceptance. The known
Git Artifact descriptor was reconstructed locally; this is not an online native
connector fetch or a persisted lifecycle/resource test.

## Source understanding and closed conclusion experiment

The next finite hypothesis replaces early Memory-shaped survey drafts with
source statements, actual modality, necessary qualifications, observations and
explicit authored relationships. A Unit-owned operation reads the complete
source, forms independently useful closed conclusion intents, selects their
exact origin Evidence, and only then expresses final Memories. No intermediate
graph becomes Evidence or a durable business record. Native field meaning and
literal code decoding remain adapter-owned; source interpretation and conclusion
formation use one generic contract.

An independent preflight exercises the complete extraction interface with
synthetic exact inputs and fake inference responses. **61/61 declared mechanical
behaviors** conform after correcting group-origination authority, bidirectional
statement/intent accounting, exact redundant-selection detection and unrelated
source-outcome binding. Shared necessary conditions do not force independent
conclusions to merge; relation-root endpoints may remain dependencies; authored
procedures and event-established lasting fixes remain expressible. Missing or
wrong Artifact bytes fail before calls; stage failure produces no completed
subset. One permitted mechanical correction keeps exact source/images, schema,
model and requested reserve unchanged, and retains initial, corrected and
accepted responses separately.

Four semantic negative controls deliberately still pass structure: hidden
independent predicates, an unsupported factual addition under a reused

test the hypothesis, not evidence of semantic acceptance.

A separate interface audit accumulated 370 structural violations across seven
returned initial/corrected intent objects. These are repeated diagnostic checks,
not 370 independent semantic defects. It falsified three interface assumptions:

claim and is not a correction.

actually asserted contribution at origin, allows grounding corrections from
the complete authorized catalog, and separates assertion mode, source commitment
and logical conditions. It does not force whole-table citations or independent
endpoint rules into each Memory. Pointer/basis accounting remains mechanical;
entailment and minimality remain independently evaluated. The original failed
responses, frozen obligations and both reviews remain evidence. A new hypothesis
requires a new finite freeze and online cohort, not relabeling this run as passed.
